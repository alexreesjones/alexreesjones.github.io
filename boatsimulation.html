Simulator

This simulator will help you evaluate how your proposed allocation compares to what would arise in a free-market equilibrium. To begin, please select the producers and consumers who will produce and consume. There must be the same number of each. Once you are ready, press the "Simulate" button. Your entries will be locked in and true values for the WTP and WTA will be calculated according to the parameters in the case. The table will be updated with this information and with information about which parties would participate in equilibrium. Any case where your suggestion is different than the equilibrium allocation is highlighted. The panel below shows a summary of market performance. To start over, click "Clear".
